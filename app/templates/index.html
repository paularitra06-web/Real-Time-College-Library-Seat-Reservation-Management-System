Welcome to the College Library

Library Hours & Access

  • Regular Timings: 7:30 AM to 12:30 AM (all days, including weekdays and weekends).
  • Digital Library & E-Resources: Accessible 24 hours a day, 7 days a week through the campus network.
  • Book Issue Limit: Issuing physical books typically stops at 7:00 PM.

Important Rules & Guidelines

  • ID Card: A valid student or staff ID card is mandatory for entry.
  • Night Library (9:00 PM to 12:30 AM): Available for late-night studying, but first-year students are generally not permitted.
  • Hostel Curfew & Attendance: Girl students usually need to return or manage attendance by around 12:00 AM / 12:30 AM with designated transport options, while boys can log attendance directly at the library or their respective blocks.
